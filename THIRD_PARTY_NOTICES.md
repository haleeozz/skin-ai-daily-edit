# Third-party references

The MIT license covers this project's original code and documentation. It does not grant rights to third-party trademarks, website designs, marketing copy, or provider services.

Product names, factual ingredient lists and short label claims are referenced for provenance and compatibility filtering. The original manufacturers retain their respective rights. Raw manufacturer pages, reviews and full marketing text are excluded from this repository. Source links and capture dates are retained; no partnership, manufacturer endorsement or clinical approval is claimed.

General skincare sources come from the American Academy of Dermatology and the US Food and Drug Administration. They support limited routine principles, not endorsement of named product options. The source registry is `data-research/guidance.json`.

Skin observations are provided by Perfect Corp.'s YouCam API, subject to its terms. The API is not redistributed. Each deployment supplies its own authorized key.

The fictional adult portrait was generated with OpenAI image generation. Its prompt and export details are in `ASSET-PROMPT.md`. It is not a clinical test image or a real user's photograph.

Dependencies retain their own licenses, as recorded in their packages and the lockfile.
